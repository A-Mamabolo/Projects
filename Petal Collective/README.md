## Petal Collective

**Live Site:** [petalcollective.co.za](https://petalcollective.co.za/)

A luxury floral design business website built for a client to help her sell and take orders for bouquets online. The site handles everything from browsing the flower collection to calculating an order total, and finally submitting an order form for payment.

### What the site does

- **About & Brand Story** – introduces the business and its floral philosophy
- **Gallery** – showcases real arrangement photography
- **Floral Collection & Pricing** – displays bouquet types (Mixed Bunch, Rose, Tulip, Lily) with size-based pricing
- **Bouquet Builder / Price Calculator** – lets customers pick a bouquet type, size, quantity, and add-ons (like filler flowers) to get an instant estimated total
- **How To Order** – a step-by-step guide from browsing to payment confirmation
- **Client Reviews** – testimonials section for social proof
- **Order Form Integration** – links out to a Google Form for order and delivery details
- **Contact & Socials** – phone, email, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok

### Challenges & What I Learned

Building this site pushed me to solve problems I hadn't tackled before:

- **Image handling** – Working through image sizing, optimization, and getting a consistent, responsive gallery layout across devices without breaking the design.
- **Building a price calculator from scratch** – Designing the logic for the bouquet builder so it dynamically updates the estimated total based on bouquet type, size, quantity, and add-ons using vanilla JavaScript.
- **Customising fonts and colours** – Translating a client's brand feel ("elegant, luxury florals") into a consistent visual system — picking, testing, and applying custom fonts and a cohesive colour palette across every section of the site.

These challenges were some of the biggest growth points in the project — turning "I don't know how to do this yet" into a shipped, working feature for a real client.
